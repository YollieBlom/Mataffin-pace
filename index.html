Coach's Tactical Briefing: Mataffin & Halls Farm

Key terrain & micro-climate factors to keep in mind this Saturday morning

1. The Litchi Canopy Trap (km 3–5)

Dropping down into Halls Farm feels effortless in the early shade. Resist banking more than your planned downhill differential—otherwise your quads will pay interest on the km 7 orchard climb back out.

2. Crocodile River Humidity (km 6–8)

Air sits still and humid under the litchi trees near the river turnaround. Your heart rate may drift 3–5 bpm higher on the return climb. Run by perceived breathing effort (RPE 8/10) rather than forcing a flat split.

3. Farm Dirt to Tarmac Transition

Compact farm dirt slightly dampens shoe energy return (~3–5 sec/km slower for the same wattage). Once you crest back onto the stadium tarmac for the final 2 km, lengthen your stride and use the firm road snap.
